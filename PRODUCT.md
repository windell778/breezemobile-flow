# PRODUCT.md — BreezeMobile Flow Intelligence

register: product

## Product purpose

Operational intelligence platform for anonymous visitor behavior. Connects campaign attribution, session recordings, and intent signals into unified visitor profiles. Not a CRM, not a BI dashboard, not a sales panel.

## Users

Primary: marketing agency account manager or in-house digital marketer reviewing campaign performance and visitor behavior mid-workday. Opens on a laptop during business hours, often between client calls or after launching a campaign. Needs to answer three questions fast: who came, what did they do, can I see the recording.

Secondary: technical operator (developer, tracking specialist) using the /tracking and /eventos views for diagnostic work.

## Brand tone

Direct, operational, precise. The product is a tool, not a brand experience. Language should be honest about data gaps ("Sin dato", "Solo página vista") without apology. No vanity metrics, no inflated labels.

## Anti-references

- No SaaS "hero metrics": big number + gradient accent + supporting stats in a card. Cliché.
- No dark-mode-because-it-looks-techy. This is a business tool used in daylight.
- No purple/indigo + lime (Linear/Vercel aesthetic). Too developer-tool.
- No navy + gold (finance aesthetic).
- No identical icon+heading+text card grids repeated endlessly.
- No glassmorphism.
- No gradient text.

## Strategic principles

1. The anonymous visitor is the protagonist. Every view leads toward a visitor profile.
2. Behavior over metrics. "Who came, from where, what did they do" beats "how many".
3. Data honesty. Missing fields shown, never hidden or imputed.
4. Context preserved across navigation. session_id and visitor_id travel with every link.
5. whatsapp_click is anonymous intent signal, never a confirmed lead or sale.
6. Tracking health is first-class. Not a hidden technical page.
7. Operators, not analysts. Fast scanning, not model-building.
